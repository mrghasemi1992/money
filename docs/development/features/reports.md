# Reports

Reports show income and expense for a period against the previous one: by category, by account, month by month, and a few insights (biggest changes, savings rate, recurring spending, largest transactions, budget record). Every chart has a table view with the same figures. Nothing is written here, so every role sees the same thing.

[Architecture](../architecture.md) explains how the pieces fit together; [conventions](../conventions.md) the rules for writing code.

## How it works

1. **One page per tab** (overview, breakdown, over time). Each resolves the period from the URL with `resolveReportPeriod`: the period itself (never past today), the previous period to compare with, and the months of the chart, all as Gregorian ranges of the viewer's calendar.
2. **Each tab runs only its own queries**, in parallel, from `src/db/reports.ts`. Every sum happens in Postgres; there is no calendar math in SQL, only the ranges.
3. **Pure helpers** in `src/helpers/report.ts` turn the totals into what the cards show: changes against the previous period, movers, savings rates, recurring spending.
4. **Each card** (`ReportCard`) has a title, an optional one-sentence finding and a chart / table switch; the charts are hand-written HTML and CSS (see [decisions](../decisions.md#9-hand-written-charts-no-chart-library)).

## Details

`/reports` (`Reports` in `src/components/reports`, pages in `src/app/(app)/reports/`). Every role reads the whole book; there is nothing to write, so no badge. Transfers count nowhere: only income and expense.

- **Three tabs, each its own page** (`REPORT_SECTIONS`, `REPORT_SECTION_PATHS`, `ReportTabs` in `src/components/report-tabs`: links under the period bar, the current one brand text with a line under it and `aria-current`): **Overview** `/reports` (`(overview)/page.tsx`: the summary, movers beside recurring, largest), **Breakdown** `/reports/breakdown` (expense categories beside income categories and accounts) and **Over time** `/reports/trends` (month by month, then savings rate beside budget record). Each page runs only its own queries (`loadReportPeriod` in `src/app/(app)/reports/period.ts` checks the session and resolves the period; `reportTitle` gives «تفکیک · گزارش‌ها»), and `Reports` takes a `section` with that tab's data (`ReportContent`). The tab links keep the period's search params, and changing the period stays on the tab. Each tab has its own `loading.tsx` (the overview's in the `(overview)` route group, so it doesn't cover the other two); `ReportsSkeleton` reads the period from the URL, so the header, period bar and tabs stay real and still while a tab loads, and only the cards are placeholders. The phone's top bar gives the tabs no back button (`tabs` on the reports `NavItem`). The empty state follows the tab: no income or expense in the period (overview, breakdown) or in the chart's months (over time).

- **Period in the URL** (`src/helpers/report.ts`, `REPORT_PARAMS`): nothing = the last 6 months (`DEFAULT_REPORT_PERIOD`), `?period=3m` / `12m`, `?month=YYYY-MM` (read with `parseMonthParam`, like the transactions page) or `?from=…&to=…` (ISO dates). `parseReportParams` reads it (a range wins over a month, a month over `period`), `reportParamsToSearch` writes it. `resolveReportPeriod` turns it into Gregorian ranges in the viewer's calendar with `monthRange`: the period (never past today), the **previous period** and the months of the chart (`ReportPeriod`).
  - 3, 6, 12 months: that many months up to and including the current one; the previous period is as many months before, and when the period runs to today it stops at today's day of the month («1–16 Mehr» against «1–16 Shahrivar»).
  - A month: that month (another calendar's month becomes the viewer's month around its middle; a future month becomes the current one), compared with the month before (cut the same way); the chart shows it with the 5 months before (`REPORT_TREND_MONTHS`), highlighted.
  - A custom range: cut to today and to at most 36 months (`REPORT_MAX_MONTHS`, an earlier start is moved up and shown); the previous period is as many days just before. Its months are cut to the range.
- **Queries** in `src/db/reports.ts` (server-only; each tab's page calls `requireUser()` and runs its own in parallel; every sum is in Postgres, no calendar math in SQL): `compareTotals` (income and expense of the period and the previous one in one statement), `categoryTotals` (both types, subcategories rolled up with `coalesce(parent_id, id)` and listed under their parent as JSON, plus `direct`, the part recorded on the category itself; transactions without a category are one row with a null id), `rangeTotals` (income and expense per month range, from a `values` list joined to the transactions, in the ranges' order) and `accountExpenseTotals` (expenses per account); for the insights `recurringExpenses`, `largestTransactions` and `budgetRecord` (below), and `categoryTotals` again for the previous period.
- **Page:** `PageHeader` with the period as its subtitle and `ReportPeriodSwitch` (`SegmentedControl`: ماه · ۳ ماه · ۶ ماه · ۱۲ ماه · بازه دلخواه, short labels on phones) as its action; `ReportPeriodBar` (the month switcher, or two DatePickers for a custom range, and «در مقایسه با …»); `ReportSummary` (income, expense, net, each with a badge for the change: a percent for income and expense, a compact amount for the net, `formatCompactMoney`; green when good news, red otherwise, and always «بیشتر» / «کمتر» in words; no badge when the previous period had none); `CategoryReport` for expense and income (a category opens to its subcategories and «بدون زیردسته»; «بدون دسته‌بندی» for uncategorized); `AccountReport` (spending by account); `MonthlyReport`. Each is a `ReportCard`: a title, a «نمودار / جدول» switch and the chart or a `DataTable` of the same figures. The empty state (no income or expense in the period) offers the add-transaction form to writers (`useAddTransaction`). Changing the period pushes the URL in a transition and shows the skeleton meanwhile.
- **Insights** (#60): five more `ReportCard`s, each with a one-sentence finding in both languages (`ReportCard`'s `summary`, between the head and the figures) and a table view. The ones that follow time use the chart's months (`period.months`: for a single month, it and the five before); the others the period. Movers, recurring and largest are on the overview; savings rate and budget record on over time. Pure helpers in `src/helpers/report.ts`, limits in `src/constants/report.ts`.
  - `MoversReport`: expense categories whose spending changed most against the previous period (`categoryMovers` joins both periods' `categoryTotals`, largest change in amount first, unchanged ones left out; `REPORT_MOVERS_COUNT` in the chart, all in the table). Each row: chip, a badge with the compact change («… بیشتر» red, «… کمتر» green), the percent («تازه» / “New” when there was nothing before), «از … به …», the subcategory that moved most (and «بدون زیردسته», `MOVER_DIRECT_ID`), and a bar for its share of the largest change, striped when spending rose.
  - `SavingsReport`: (income − expense) ÷ income per month (`savingsRate`, null without income: «—», «بدون درآمد»), a bar from a zero line toward the end (royal) or the start (red, striped), stopped at −100% (`REPORT_SAVINGS_FLOOR`; the rate is written out), and a dashed line for all the months together (total income and expense, not an average of rates). Positions are CSS percentages with logical insets, so it flips with `dir`.
  - `RecurringReport`: expense descriptions in more than half of the months, at least 2 (`recurringMinMonths`, `REPORT_RECURRING_SHARE`). `recurringExpenses` groups in SQL by the description folded like the search (lowercase, «ي» «ى» «ك» as Persian, ZWNJ and runs of spaces as one space), keeps the spelling and category most rows have (`mode()`), and counts the months it appears in from the same `values` list as `rangeTotals`; the most months first, then the most spent, `REPORT_RECURRING_COUNT`. Each row: the category's tile and name, «۵ از ۶ ماه» with a strip of months, what it costs in a month it appears; it links to `/transactions` searching for the description over those months (`recurringTransactionsHref`). Money has no payee field, so descriptions stand in for payees. A period under two months says to pick a longer one.
  - `LargestReport`: the `REPORT_LARGEST_COUNT` largest expenses and incomes (`largestTransactions`, `row_number()` per type), beside each other when wide; each row links to `/transactions` on its day, type and category (or `unknown=1`, `largeTransactionHref`).
  - `BudgetRecordReport`: per budgeted category, each month's spending against the limit (`budgetRecord`, subcategories rolled up) in the budget colors (within as a soft fill, so near (solid amber with a dot) and over (red, striped) stand out), a cell per month sharing the row and «۴ از ۶ ماه در سقف» (`monthsWithinBudget`: near still counts as within); the table gives each month as a percent of the limit. Budgets keep no history, so every month is held to today's limit, and the card says so.
- **Charts are hand-written** (no chart library): see Charts in [design system](design-system.md).

## Why there is no chart library

- **Charts:** hand-written React components (HTML and CSS, SVG only for the net line), no chart library: the two chart forms are simple, CSS grid and logical properties flip them with `dir`, colors are tokens so both themes need no JavaScript, all text uses our formatting (digits, calendars, units, Dana) and they render on the server without measuring. If later charts need scales, axes or zooming beyond this, add `d3-scale` or visx for the math and keep our own markup.
  - **Decision (#60, reviewed for the report insights): still no chart library.** None of the insights needs what the hand-written charts can't do: biggest movers and largest transactions are ranked bars, the savings rate is one bar per month around a zero line (a CSS position, no axis ticks), recurring spending and the budget record are grids of months. What would need more: a trend over many points with a continuous time axis, zoom or brushing (for example daily balances over years), or several lines whose labels must avoid each other. Compared for that day (minified + gzipped, measured with esbuild): **`d3-scale`** about 9 KB (scales and `ticks()` only, our own markup, so RTL, tokens, Dana and server rendering stay as they are); **visx** (`@visx/scale` + `shape` + `axis` + `group`) about 24 KB, SVG components positioned by x coordinates that RTL has to mirror by hand (reversed ranges or a transform), axis text drawn in SVG outside our type roles, and responsive sizes measured in the browser (`ParentSize`), so the first server render has no width. When that chart comes, add `d3-scale` and keep the markup ours.
